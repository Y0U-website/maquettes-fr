Orthodontics for everyone

An individualized approach for treatments tailored to your needs

The objective of orthodontics is aesthetic, but also functional. It is used to correct poorly positioned teeth, discrepancies between the jaws, and functions that are difficult to perform such as chewing, swallowing, breathing and speaking.

You can request an orthodontic consultation yourself without a prescription or prescription from your dentist or doctor. Orthodontic treatment can start at any age. Most often it is between 6 and 12 years old.
