Dr. Guillaume Dutreuil

A graduate of the Bordeaux II Faculty, Dr. Dutreuil focuses his practice on adhesive, conservative, and biomimetic dentistry . This modern approach aims to preserve the integrity of your natural teeth as much as possible while restoring function and aesthetics using biocompatible materials.

✦ Implantology

Dental implant placement and fixed prosthetic rehabilitations to replace missing teeth.

✦ Aesthetics

Smile design: ceramic veneers, metal-free crowns, and professional teeth whitening.

✦ Aligners

Clear aligner orthodontic treatment (Invisalign) for discreet teeth straightening.
