Your first visit

Getting here

Our orthodontic practices in Clermont-Ferrand are accessible by tram (Jaude stop) with nearby car parks (parking Jaude, parking des Salins).

First consultation

Your first consultation with the orthodontist lasts approximately 30 minutes. It includes a clinical examination, radiographic assessment and presentation of a tailored treatment plan.

Documents to bring

Please bring your health insurance card, a form of ID, your referral letter (if sent by your dentist) and any recent X-rays.

Opening hours

Our practices welcome you Monday to Friday, 9 am to 7 pm. Appointments can be made by phone or online.

Everything about orthodontics

It is recommended to visit an orthodontist from the age of 6–7. An early assessment can detect growth discrepancies and allow timely intervention. However, there is no age limit: adults can also benefit from orthodontic treatment.

Treatment duration varies depending on the complexity of the case, generally between 12 and 24 months. During your first consultation, your orthodontist will provide a precise estimate of the treatment duration and the number of appointments required.

Clear aligners are removable and nearly invisible, ideal for mild to moderate cases. Traditional brackets (metal or ceramic) are fixed and can treat the most complex cases. Your orthodontist will recommend the most suitable solution for you.

In France, orthodontic treatment is covered by the national health system for patients under 16, subject to prior approval. Beyond that age, supplementary insurance may contribute. A detailed estimate will be provided during your first consultation.

Both our practices are located in the city centre of Clermont-Ferrand, near Place de Jaude. The tram (lines A and B, Jaude stop) drops you just a few minutes walk away. By car, the Jaude and Salins car parks are the closest options.

Some mild discomfort may be felt in the first few days after the appliance is fitted or following an adjustment. This sensation is normal and subsides quickly. Your orthodontist will give you all the advice you need for optimal comfort throughout treatment.
