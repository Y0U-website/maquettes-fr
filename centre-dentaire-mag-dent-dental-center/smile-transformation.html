Invisalign is a straightening system that uses a series of custom-made removable aligners. We often use the term “invisible or clear braces” because these translucent aligners are nearly invisible when they are worn.

No more need to have metallic braces to align your teeth. Comfortable, clear and removable – Invisalign transforms your smile without disrupting your life. It is difficult to imagine that those simple aligners could transform your smile but it is something that over 3 million people worldwide have already discovered.

Dental implant technology has changed significantly over the years. No need for the long wait, a new alternative is now available. We can now provide in most cases, fixed dentures the same day of surgery. This technique requires a team of skilled professionals. The process is performed with the help of our certified oral and maxillofacial surgeons.

All-on-4 technique A minimum of 4 implants are placed in the jaw and the temporary prosthesis is fabricated on-site and inserted immediately. The prosthesis is screwed into the implants, which means the teeth are fixed and not removed in most cases the same day of surgery.
