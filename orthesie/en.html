Orthésie Laboratory: Excellence serving digital and traditional orthodontics

The Smile Architect: When expert craftsmanship meets digital precision.

Orthésie Laboratory is not just a production unit; it is a hybrid expertise hub. Founded by Stéphane Arzoumanian, an expert trained in fundamental techniques (Tweed, Planas) and a digital pioneer, our laboratory anticipated the industry’s technological shift. Today, we combine half a century of clinical experience with the power of Industry 4.0. As holders of international patents, we have reinvented appliance design to offer practitioners absolute reliability and investors a clear vision: a laboratory that defines tomorrow’s standards.

Prosthetic excellence, from classic to avant-garde.

From traditional resin to laser micro-fusion expanders, discover a range that covers all your treatment plans.

Forget the structural weak points of traditional appliances. Thanks to our mastery of metal 3D printing (laser micro-fusion) and our global patents, we have pushed the boundaries of mechanical physics.

Unlike traditional assemblies, the molar band and the tube are printed as a single, continuous piece of material.

Anatomical design: The tube is not simply "placed" on the band; it is its natural extension, offering unparalleled resistance to torsion.

Reduced bulk: A level of fineness impossible to achieve manually, for immediate patient comfort.

Get help

What are your manufacturing lead times?

We are committed to controlled lead times to streamline your clinical schedule: 8 business days for conventional orthodontics (Plates, arches, trays). 15 business days for complex devices (Digital appliances, PUL, Rods, Planas Tracks).

What is your delivery area?

We cover the entire national territory (Mainland France and overseas territories) as well as international locations. Whether your practice is located nearby or halfway across the world, our logistics partners ensure fast and secure delivery.

Is it possible to treat urgent cases?

Absolutely. We know that clinical reality sometimes involves the unexpected (loss, breakage, short deadlines). Simply contact us before sending: we will prioritize your order to reduce lead times to the strict technical minimum.

Which intra-oral scanners are you compatible with?

We are an "open" laboratory. We process .STL / .PLY / .OBJ files from all cameras on the market (iTero, 3Shape Trios, Medit, Dexis, Carestream...). Our digital workflow is constantly updated to integrate the latest technologies as soon as they are released.

Do you ensure material traceability?

Health safety is our priority. Each device delivered is accompanied by its individual traceability sheet, detailing the origin and compliance of the materials used (resins, metals, components), in accordance with European regulations.
